# Visitor Scoring Rubric

Each persona agent scores the site 0-10 per dimension, in character, based only
on what the site actually shows. Anchors: 0-3 = would leave / distrust,
4-6 = neutral, unconvinced, 7-8 = convinced with minor doubts,
9-10 = would contact today. Score what you experienced, not what you assume
was intended. Do not grade on a curve; a 9+ must be earned by something
specific you can quote or point to.

## Dimensions (score each 0-10 with a one-sentence justification)

1. **First impression and trust** — within 10 seconds, does this person look
   real, professional, and safe to pay?
2. **Clarity of offer** — do I understand what he does FOR ME without
   re-reading?
3. **Relevance to my situation** — does any part of the page speak to my
   specific problem? (persona-specific)
4. **Credibility and proof** — portfolio, credentials, real projects: do I
   believe it, and can I verify any of it?
5. **Language and readability** — natural, warm Thai; scannable; no jargon I
   don't know; nothing that reads translated or AI-generated.
6. **Design quality** — modern, alive, intentional; would I judge his web
   skills positively from his own site?
7. **Experience on my device** — layout, tap targets, reading comfort on the
   device this persona uses.
8. **Ease of acting** — how much friction between "I'm interested" and
   "I sent a LINE message"? Is what to say clear?
9. **Objection handling** — are MY doubts (persona-specific) addressed
   somewhere I actually found?
10. **Differentiation** — vs Fastwork freelancers, agencies, a friend's
    nephew, or doing it myself with AI: why him?

## Required verdict block (after the scores)

- **Total** (sum /100)
- **Would you send a LINE message today?** yes / maybe / no, with the one
  sentence that most influenced the decision (quote it) and the one thing
  that most held you back.
- **Top 3 improvements** ranked, each tied to a specific place on a specific
  page.
- **The moment you almost left** — where and why, if it happened.

## Ground rules for agents

- Stay in character; react to the actual Thai copy as your persona would.
- Browse every page (/, /work, /contact, /fix-ai-website, 404 optional) at
  your persona's device width before scoring.
- Image placeholders: judge the labeled intent (a real photo/screenshot will
  replace it), but note when a missing real image materially hurts trust for
  your persona.
- Quote exact lines when praising or criticizing copy.
