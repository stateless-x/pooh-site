# Context: Pooh site

Freelance client-acquisition site for ภูรินท์ บุรีวงศ์ (Pooh) at pooh.fyi. Two language editions share one site; each edition has its own audience, voice, and success event.

## Glossary

### 67 dance
The 6.7 second show started by the `67` key on the mascot stage: dark page, coloured lights, the bear doing the 6 7 hand gesture faster and faster, and a big 3D numeral that grows and bursts.
- **Not to be confused with:** a click mood, which is a short reaction to poking the bear and leaves the page light.

### APAC-economics framing
The English edition's value argument: smart companies already hire senior APAC talent deliberately, so the buyer gets the same senior quality while their budget goes further. Always relative, never absolute.
- **Not to be confused with:** pricing — no rates, figures, or amounts ever appear; the framing is banned from using the words "cheap", "affordable", "low-cost", or "budget-friendly".

### English edition
The native English rewrite of the site under `/en/`, written from scratch for the international visitor — not a translation of the Thai copy. Covers every page except the blog.
- **Not to be confused with:** the Thai edition — same person, same facts, different voice and different primary contact channel.

### International visitor
The English edition's reader: either a foreign owner operating in or around Thailand, or a remote international client evaluating an engineer from abroad. Both are driven to email.

### Language switcher
The visible TH/EN control in the navigation bar and hamburger menu. A visitor's explicit choice always wins over browser-language detection and is remembered.

### Mascot stage
The interactive three.js bear in the home hero of both editions, which loads behind a still poster.
- **Not to be confused with:** the mascot illustrations, which are flat images used elsewhere on the site.

### Success event (English)
An international visitor sends an email to askpurin@pm.me. LINE remains available as a secondary channel on the English contact page only.
- **Not to be confused with:** the Thai success event — a message to the personal LINE.

### Thai edition
The existing Thai site at the root URLs, unchanged. Thai is the fallback language when detection cannot decide.

### Update notice
The dated strip above the header on every page, which opens a modal listing what the owner is working on and learning. It is edited by hand when the month changes.
- **Not to be confused with:** a news feed or the planned blog; it has one current entry and no history.

### Voice (English)
The same warm, honest, plain first-person voice as the Thai edition, in grade-8 English, carried by persuasion structure (clear offer, handled objection, one email CTA) rather than persuasive tone (formerly "full wolf", rejected as arrogant — see ADR 0002). Grade 8 means natural sentence variety and the vocabulary a normal adult reader expects, not jargon, longer paragraphs, or formality. May name real technical things when they build trust. Identity is stated plainly: a freelance developer based in Bangkok, Thailand, taking remote work.
- **Not to be confused with:** the Thai voice — same warmth, but Thai additionally bans all technical vocabulary.
